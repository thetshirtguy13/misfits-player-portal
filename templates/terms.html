Purpose

Misfits Player Development provides baseball training, workout tracking, statistics, team tools, and educational content. It is not medical care and does not replace instruction from a qualified coach or healthcare professional.

Youth accounts

A parent or guardian must approve a youth player account. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising use of the service and determining whether a workout is appropriate for their player.

Training safety

Players should stop activity for pain, dizziness, or injury concerns. Pitching and throwing workloads should follow current coach-approved age and rest guidance.

Account responsibilities

Users must keep passwords private, provide accurate information, and use only accounts and team data they are authorized to access.

Statistics and imports

Imported or manually entered statistics may contain errors. Users are responsible for reviewing imported GameChanger data before relying on it.

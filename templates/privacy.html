Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Information collected

The app can store account information, player age group and position, team membership, workout completions and notes, baseball statistics, parent/guardian consent records, and security/audit events.

How information is used

Information is used to provide player-development features, show progress to authorized players, parents, and coaches, manage teams, protect accounts, and support account requests.

Youth privacy

Player accounts are designed to require parent or guardian consent before development progress can be recorded. Player information is not intended to be public. Coaches can view a player only when the coach and player share an approved team membership.

Sharing

The app does not include advertising or sale of personal information. Hosting, database, and email providers may process information only as necessary to operate the service.

Retention and control

Users can export their account data and request deletion from the Account page. Operational backups may retain deleted records for a limited period according to the hosting provider’s backup policy.

Security

Passwords are stored as hashes. The application uses CSRF protection, rate limiting, secure session settings, access controls, and security headers. Production deployment must use HTTPS and securely configured environment variables.

Before public launch, replace this section with the organization’s official privacy contact email and mailing address.
